How accurate is it?

What the check marks mean
  • Gray ✓ — sent, no open detected yet.
  • Green ✓✓ — opened. Click it on a sent message to see when and in what app.
  • Dashed amber ✓✓ — delivered to a privacy-protecting mail app (Apple Mail, Proton) that loads every email automatically, so a real read can't be confirmed.
What Seen filters out
  • You viewing your own sent email in Gmail in this browser (blocked before it even loads).
  • Apple Mail Privacy Protection, Proton and Gmail preloading images on delivery.
  • Corporate security scanners that open emails on delivery.
  • Repeat loads within a few minutes (counted as one open).
When an open can be missed or miscounted
  • The recipient has images turned off, or reads in plain text.
  • You open your own sent email on your phone (the extension isn't there to say "that was me").
  • Group emails: Seen knows the email was opened, not by whom.
  • Scheduled sends aren't tracked (Gmail sends them from its servers later).
  • Repeat opens can be undercounted because Google caches images.
